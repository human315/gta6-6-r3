Sunhaven is a complete open-world crime game that runs in a desktop browser from
static files, with no network requests: explore a sunny coastal city on foot and
by car, steal cars, outrun the Sunhaven PD under a five-star wanted system, and
run four story missions for fixer Lena Ortiz — ending in the Big Score at the
Sunhaven Trust Bank.

## Play

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 and press Space to start.

## Controls

- **WASD / arrows** — walk (Shift to run) or drive
- **E** — steal / enter a car, get out again
- **Mouse** — aim, **click** — shoot
- **Space** — handbrake while driving, confirm on menus
- **P / Esc** — pause, **M** — mute

## What is inside

- A 3200×2400 open city: grid avenues, buildings, parks, parking lots, a beach
  and a pier, with traffic that keeps its lane and pedestrians on the sidewalks
- Car stealing with a wanted level that rises with every crime and cools off
  when you stay out of sight; foot cops and police cruisers hunt you down
- Four story missions with a cash payout each, a pistol with ammo, armed bank
  guards, and a win screen when the Big Score lands
- HUD with cash, health, ammo, wanted stars, objective text and a live minimap
- Save / continue in localStorage, pause, mute, and fully synthesized WebAudio
  sound: engine, sirens, gunshots and a bass groove — no audio files at all

Everything is original: the city of Sunhaven, Kai Reyes, Lena Ortiz and Dario
Kane, the art and the sounds were made for this game. Nothing is taken from
any commercial game.
